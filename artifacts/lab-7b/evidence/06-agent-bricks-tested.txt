Why the difference
------------------
The Knowledge Assistant Settings pane exposes exactly two fields: "Instructions
(optional)" and "Description". There is no metadata-filter control. The brick
queries the whole index. The access control that the hand-built agent implements
as a query-time filter has no equivalent in the brick's configuration surface.
