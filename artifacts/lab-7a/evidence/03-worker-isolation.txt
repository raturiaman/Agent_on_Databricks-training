  task: A 'plus' tier customer is returning 25 units of a single seating product. Would they pay a return shipping fee?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  No — they would not pay a return shipping fee.  Although DOC-001
  states that plus tier customers are normally charged a flat 12 GBP
  collection fee for returns, DOC-007 specifies that orders of 20 units
  or more of a single seating product qualify as a "fit-out order," and
  fit-out orders receive **free collection regardless of customer
  tier**. Since this customer is returning 25 units of a single seating
  product, the order qualifies as a fit-out order, and the free
  collection provision overrides the standard tier-based fee.

  cited: ['DOC-001', 'DOC-007', 'DOC-005']
