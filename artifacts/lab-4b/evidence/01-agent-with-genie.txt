## Is there a policy reason they'd have stopped ordering?

At 35–40 units each, both orders qualify as **fit-out orders** (20+ units of a single seating product) per policy DOC-007. Two relevant clauses could plausibly explain a pause rather than churn:

- **Staged delivery allowance**: Fit-out orders can be delivered in staged batches at the customer's request, within 8 weeks of the original order — so Nordic Office Group may simply be in a fulfillment/installation cycle rather than sourcing from elsewhere.
- **Extended 60-day return window** on fit-out orders means they have until roughly mid-October to review/return, which could naturally delay a reorder decision until that window closes.
- **No cancellation charge was triggered** — the 15% restocking fee for cancelling a fit-out order after production begins doesn't apply here since nothing was cancelled.

There's no policy (billing, returns, or credit hold) in the documents indicating friction that would have driven this customer away — payment terms (DOC-005) only affect Premier-tier invoicing/credit checks, and there's no sign of a credit hold or dispute in the order data.

**Bottom line:** This looks like a normal post-fit-out lull for a bulk buyer rather than a lost account. I'd recommend a quick check-in with Nordic Office Group to confirm their next reorder timing — if they've gone quiet beyond the typical cycle after a 40+60-unit fit-out, that's the point to flag as a genuine risk.
